My role Product Designer
The brief

Caree wanted to test its idea on the market quickly. I designed an MVP spanning three connected products across mobile and desktop: a CRM, an embeddable booking widget and a public website.

The challenge was to create a coherent experience across them while keeping the scope focused enough to move quickly from idea to market.

Selected work

Mapping the product ecosystem

I mapped how the customer-facing booking experience connected to the business-side CRM. With speed driving the MVP, this gave us a shared view of the ecosystem and helped us decide what was essential for launch and what could move to the backlog.

One experience, two sides

A booking starts with the customer, but it doesn't end there. I designed the core journey across both sides of the product, from finding and booking an appointment to receiving and managing it in the CRM.
