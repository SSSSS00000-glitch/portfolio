The brief

Baemingo served different brands, business models and touchpoints, from back office tools and point of sale to self service and customer facing products. The challenge was creating one flexible system without making every experience feel the same.

Selected work・White label products

Designing for many brands without designing everything twice

Baemingo was built as a white label product used across different restaurant brands including Vigårda, Babas and Oh Poké. I designed a flexible customer journey that could adapt to different identities, content and menus while keeping the underlying experience consistent.

The impact
+10% Average order value across the product
+60% Average bill for Baba's Burgers

Beamingo now powers ordering for 4,000+ restaurants — built on the flexible, multi-brand system I helped design.
