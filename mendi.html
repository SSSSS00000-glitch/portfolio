My role

I was brought in to turn a complex and fragmented experience into something clear, trustworthy and easy to come back to.

I led end-to-end product design and evolved the brand identity so the product and brand worked as one. I shipped a new design system and core flows, ran research and testing, and kept accessibility and content in scope.

My reflection

Growing with the product

Mendi changed how I think as a designer. I stopped looking at design as a series of features to design and started looking at the product as a whole.

What should we build? What should we leave out? What do users actually need? And how can we learn quickly enough to know the difference?

I became more comfortable making decisions with incomplete information, connecting user needs with product, technology and business, and thinking beyond the next release.

Design decisions are product decisions.
